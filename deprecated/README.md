# Deprecated window-manager configs

These files are **not imported** by the flake. They preserve the previous
yabai/skhd (Darwin) and Hyprland (NixOS) stacks, including special workspaces
(`fullscreen`, `steam`, `battlenet`).

To restore, re-import the relevant files and undo the Nehir/niri wiring in
`config/` and `modules/home/`.
